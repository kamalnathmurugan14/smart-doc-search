# 5-step demo script (≈ 3 minutes)

1. **Start** backend + frontend (README quick start). Open the app – show the onboarding card.
2. **Index**: Settings → *Re-index*; show the progress bar and toast. Click it again → everything is *skipped* (incremental).
3. **Search**: query `renewal contract` in *keyword* mode, then `car repair` in *semantic* (finds the automobile note). Switch to *hybrid* and move the slider.
4. **Explain**: open *Explain* on a result – walk through term tf/idf/contribution, cosine, and the hybrid formula. Filter by the *Invoice* tag.
5. **Duplicates**: open the page – show the exact copy of the service agreement. Upload an edited copy in the Explorer, re-index, refresh → near duplicate.
